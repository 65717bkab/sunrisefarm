Good to know

How does payment work?

You pay the full price by M-Pesa or card when you reserve. We then confirm collection or delivery with you.

Can I visit the farm first?

Yes. Use Book a farm visit, pick a day and time, and we confirm by phone.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa, where a prompt appears on your phone, or by Visa or Mastercard through Paystack. We never see your card details.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. After you pay you can download a receipt with your reference number.

Do you deliver?

Delivery can be arranged on request. Call or WhatsApp us to ask.

Our Story

Nurtured with Nature, Shared with Heart.

At Sunrise Farm, we believe that farming is more than a business — it’s a passion, a responsibility, and a way to nourish our communities.

Rooted in a love for agriculture, we are dedicated to raising quality poultry and providing reliable products to customers and fellow farmers. From improved kienyeji chicks to our growing range of poultry products, we strive to combine responsible farming practices with care, dedication, and a commitment to continuous improvement.

Our journey is guided by a simple belief: when we care for our animals, respect nature, and put our customers first, we create something meaningful.

As we grow, our vision is to support fellow farmers, contribute to food security, and build lasting relationships founded on trust and quality.

Sunrise Farm — growing with purpose, nurturing with care, and sharing the rewards of farming with heart.

Luqman YusufOwner & CEO
